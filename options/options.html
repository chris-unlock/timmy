Address Bar Shortcut

You can search Gmail without ever opening the extension popup:

  1. Click your Chrome address bar (or press Ctrl+L / Cmd+L).
  2. Type t and hit Space or Tab.
  3. Type your search query (e.g. t invoice 2026).
  4. Press Enter to execute your default preset (e.g. Search Entire Mailbox).
  5. Or use the ↓ Down Arrow to pick any other preset suggestion.
